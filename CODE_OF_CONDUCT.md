# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Ultra Health to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the people who use the app.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing anyone's private information, **including health data**, without explicit
  permission.
- Unsolicited medical advice aimed at other contributors.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that don't follow this Code of Conduct, and may block contributors for
behaviour they consider inappropriate.

Report problems privately to the maintainer through GitHub
([@rmcguiness](https://github.com/rmcguiness)). Reports are handled confidentially.
